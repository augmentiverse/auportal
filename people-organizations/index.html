01 / Intellectual references

Research and perspectives.

NE
Augmentiverse framework

Noureddine Elmqaddem

Author of the 2026 iJET reference article. The publisher lists École des Sciences de l’information (ESI), Rabat, Morocco, as the article affiliation.

Read the source ↗
PM
Reality–virtuality taxonomy

Paul Milgram & Fumio Kishino

Their 1994 taxonomy provides a foundational way to describe mixed-reality displays. The related SPIE article also credits Haruo Takemura and Akira Utsumi.

Read the source ↗
MW
Ubiquitous computing

Mark Weiser

The Computer for the 21st Century (1991) describes computation integrated into everyday environments.

Read the source ↗
SG
Spatial computing

Simon Greenwold

His 2003 MIT thesis is a conceptual reference for computing with real objects and spaces.

Read the source ↗
NT
Open standards advocacy

Neil Trevett

Included in the original site for his work with Khronos and cross-industry interoperability. Consult Khronos for current leadership information.

Read the source ↗
MB
Industry commentary

Matthew Ball

His essay on spatial computing and the metaverse provides a perspective on competing terminology. It is commentary, distinct from a technical specification.
