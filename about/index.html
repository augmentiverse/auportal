An independent reference resource

Augmentiverse.org presents an academic and conceptual framework for an AR-first path to shared spatial reality. The site connects explanations to research and primary specifications, making the relationships between technologies easier to explore.

It is not a standards body, commercial platform or claim of industry-wide consensus. Names of organisations and products identify relevant work; they do not imply endorsement.

The framework’s author

Noureddine Elmqaddem is the author of the reference article. Its publisher identifies École des Sciences de l’information (ESI), Rabat, Morocco, as his affiliation for that publication.

  • Author and publication record↗online-journals.org

Editorial approach

Separate established facts, research propositions, interpretations and future scenarios. Give standards their actual status and version. Avoid invented quotations, affiliations and unsourced market statistics. Preserve the original sources when making corrections.

The original English site and its catalogue have been preserved in the archive. The revised pages consolidate their useful content and link to the full records. Some legacy bibliographic and product details still require editorial verification.

Languages and accessibility
