AR Smart Glasses and Standards-Led Convergence for Interoperability and Spatial Computing.

Noureddine Elmqaddem · pp. 59–72

Read the publication ↗Research & citation →

What the paper contributes

The article brings together an AR-first trajectory, open standards and spatial computing. It discusses how runtime interfaces, content interchange, portable identity and infrastructure can contribute to a shared spatial layer.

The paper’s architectural direction is a proposal. Components have their own specifications and implementations; their existence does not establish seamless interoperability or a fully deployed Augmentiverse.

The intellectual foundations

The original site connects the framework to immersive display research, ubiquitous computing and spatial computing. These sources illuminate different questions: how experiences are classified, how computing fits everyday activity, and how software refers to space.
